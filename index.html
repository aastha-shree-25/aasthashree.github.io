04 / How I work

From problem
to product.

I run the whole arc — from framing the real problem to proving the product moved the business.

01

Frame the problem

User interviews and working groups to separate the real problem from the requested feature.

02

Define what matters

Vision, OKRs, and a roadmap; PRDs and stories with clear acceptance criteria; MoSCoW to prioritise.

03

Ship & learn

Agile delivery, experimentation, and a bias to redirect effort to the highest-ROI work.

04

Land it & measure impact

Rollout, enablement, and feedback loops; usage, engagement, and cost-avoidance tracked on dashboards.
